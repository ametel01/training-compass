# Training Compass

Training Compass is a private, local-first iPhone training app. The current executable contains protected local training workflows plus an opt-in Health connection: TMs manages kilogram Training Maxes and per-lift Loading Increments, reviews completed-cycle progression proposals, and records independent decisions; Cycle maintains the reusable Schedule Template, prepares Draft Training Cycles, and activates one durable cycle with immutable 5/3/1 prescriptions. Today exposes prescribed, failed, omitted, and Additional Set work, requires explicit confirmation to complete and display a planned-versus-actual Session, and supports correction-backed reopening of completed work. Health access is optional and imports source-aware Health Workouts into the reconstructible store without blocking local planning or logging. The approved Unified Events and Enrichment milestone lets a completed 5/3/1 Session be explicitly linked one-to-one with an unlinked external Health Workout; the unified Training Event is counted once while retaining both sources, provenance, disagreements, late detail, recovery, and an explicit unlink path. Opening a workout detail can request route access on demand and display only locally retained, simplified route geometry; ordinary import and enrichment never prefetch routes.

The app targets stable iOS 26 with Swift 6 strict concurrency. Today becomes available after an Active Training Cycle has a Session scheduled for the current date, while Cycle becomes available after the default schedule's lifts are configured in TMs. Progress shows transparent per-lift e1RM history derived from eligible normal-week Primary Plus Set Results and remains explainable when no eligible data exists. Health presents source-aware Recovery Evidence and independent Personal Recovery Baselines, with an optional neutral Recovery Guidance self-check that is withheld unless current, successful, comparable evidence from at least two families is available; it never scores, diagnoses, predicts, or prescribes. TMs also provides an inspectable, versioned Training Compass Export with a sensitive-data confirmation, SHA-256 integrity digest, optional source-labelled HealthKit reference material, and temporary-file cleanup after sharing. A validated export can be restored through an export-first, all-or-nothing replacement import; HealthKit reference material is never installed as live data.
