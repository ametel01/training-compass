# Personal Fitness Training

The language for a private iPhone app that combines Apple Health workout history with user-defined 5/3/1 strength sessions.

## Language

**Training Schedule**:
The user-defined arrangement of 5/3/1 Sessions across a complete Training Cycle. The app does not prescribe a canonical 5/3/1 template.
_Avoid_: 5/3/1 program, app-generated program

**Default Schedule**:
Four weekly 5/3/1 Sessions: Monday squat/bench, Tuesday overhead press/Romanian deadlift, Thursday bench/squat, and Friday deadlift/overhead press, with the first lift Primary and the second Assistance.
_Avoid_: mandatory schedule, canonical 5/3/1 schedule

**Training Cycle**:
A planned block consisting of three normal Training Weeks, with a Deload Week appended after every second normal block.
_Avoid_: calendar month, fixed four-week cycle

**Training Week**:
One progression stage within a Training Cycle, normally containing four 5/3/1 Sessions.
_Avoid_: calendar week

**Deload Week**:
The lower-intensity week appended after every second three-week block, using sets of five at 40%, 50%, and 60% of Training Max.
_Avoid_: rest week, fourth week

**5/3/1 Session**:
A manually entered strength-training session containing one Primary Lift and its paired Assistance Lift, with weights calculated from user-configured Training Maxes.
_Avoid_: Health workout, automatic workout

**Primary Lift**:
The main movement in a 5/3/1 Session, performed with the Training Week's three prescribed percentage sets and final Plus Set.
_Avoid_: assistance movement, accessory exercise

**Assistance Lift**:
The secondary movement paired with a Primary Lift in a 5/3/1 Session, normally prescribed as five sets of ten at 65% of its own Training Max.
_Avoid_: Health workout, unstructured light weights

**Plus Set**:
The final set of a normal 5/3/1 Session, with a minimum target and additional repetitions recorded as performance permits.
_Avoid_: fixed-repetition set, max test

**Health Workout**:
Any training session supplied through Apple Health, regardless of workout type or the device or app that originally recorded it.
_Avoid_: Cardio session, Zone 1 session

**Recovery Evidence**:
Available Apple Health measurements, such as sleep, resting heart rate, and heart-rate variability, shown with their underlying trends.
_Avoid_: readiness score, medical diagnosis

**Recovery Guidance**:
An explained, advisory interpretation of Recovery Evidence that never changes or postpones training without user confirmation.
_Avoid_: automatic adaptation, readiness score

**Training Max (TM)**:
The configurable per-lift reference weight from which a 5/3/1 Session's prescribed weights are calculated.
_Avoid_: one-rep max, prescribed weight

**Estimated One-Rep Max (e1RM)**:
An estimate of maximal strength inferred from performance recorded during 5/3/1 Sessions.
_Avoid_: Training Max, actual one-rep max
