# Establish the recovery interpretation envelope

Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

According to high-trust primary research and authoritative health guidance, what cautious claims may this non-medical personal training app make when a user's sleep duration or consistency, resting heart rate, or HRV SDNN differs from that user's recent baseline? Establish the evidentiary limits of individual and conflicting signals, whether any transparent combination rules are defensible, and the language that avoids diagnosis, causal claims, or prescriptive training changes.

## Answer

Training Compass may state exactly how recorded sleep, resting heart rate, and HealthKit HRV SDNN compare with the user's recent median and middle-half band, then neutrally invite the user to consider those measurements alongside how they feel. The band is descriptive, not a normal or anomaly range, and the two-signal minimum is a presentation guardrail rather than a validated classifier. No reviewed evidence supports converting individual signals, alignment, or a vote into readiness, recovery, diagnosis, risk, or a concrete training change; aligned, conflicting, missing, stale, and source-incomparable evidence must remain separately visible. Full evidence, safe language, and implementation requirements: [Recovery interpretation envelope](../../../docs/research/recovery-interpretation-envelope.md).
