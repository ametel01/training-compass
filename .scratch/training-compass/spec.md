# Build Training Compass

Status: ready-for-agent

## Problem Statement

The owner needs one private iPhone app that makes daily strength training, recent workout history, running performance, and recovery context understandable without scattering the work across a spreadsheet, a generic workout logger, and several Apple Health views. The app must plan and log the owner's configurable 5/3/1 work precisely, continuously reconcile the Health Workouts and Recovery Evidence already available through Apple Health, and explain every derived observation well enough that the owner can inspect its source and limitations.

Existing products do not preserve the distinctions that matter for this workflow. Planned 5/3/1 Sessions are not the same records as Health Workouts; a completed local Session and a linked Health Workout may describe one Training Event without either source surrendering authority. Training Maxes, Set Prescriptions, Set Results, Deload cadence, and audited corrections require stronger local semantics than a HealthKit workout summary can represent. Recovery measurements can provide useful context, but they must not become an opaque readiness score, diagnosis, risk claim, performance prediction, or automatic training prescription.

The app will hold sensitive, irreplaceable training history on one personally managed iPhone. Local training must remain available without Health access, connectivity, or fresh reconciliation. Imported data must remain source-aware and eventually consistent. Owner-created data needs explicit backup, export, replacement-import, migration, and erasure guarantees. The implementation must also stay responsive and energy-conscious at a generous single-user data scale and remain maintainable under the accepted free Personal Team signing lifecycle.

## Solution

Build Training Compass as an on-device-only native SwiftUI app for one owner. Organize daily use around four permanent destinations: Today, Cycle, Progress, and TMs. Let the owner configure lifts, Training Maxes, Loading Increments, and a reusable Schedule Template; construct Draft and Active Training Cycles; view exact immutable prescriptions; log actual, failed, omitted, and additional sets; finish or abandon cycles explicitly; inspect e1RM evidence; and accept or reject each Training Max Proposal.

Import authorized Apple Health workouts, sleep, resting heart rate, HRV SDNN, and available workout enrichment into a deletion-aware HealthKit Mirror. Keep every Health Data Stream independently observable and recoverable. Present cached information immediately, reconcile opportunistically in the background and on foreground entry, and give the owner explicit Health Data Refresh and Health Data Rebuild controls without ever blocking local 5/3/1 work.

Represent each user-visible occurrence as a Training Event containing a Health Workout, a local 5/3/1 Session, or an explicit one-to-one link between them. Preserve source authority and provenance. Automatically identify only Training Compass's own versioned HealthKit Write-backs; require the owner to link any externally recorded Health Workout.

Provide transparent, activity-appropriate insights rather than combined scores. Show a Rolling Workout Overview, per-lift e1RM history, running-only volume and Comparable Run observations, and source-aware Recovery Evidence against Personal Recovery Baselines. Offer strictly neutral Recovery Guidance only when its evidence-family and freshness gates are satisfied, and never let an insight change a Training Schedule or Training Max.

Store Locally Authoritative Data and reconstructible HealthKit data separately, protect both stores, back up only the authoritative store through ordinary Apple device backup, and provide a versioned Training Compass Export for explicit portable recovery. Deliver the app through an engineering foundation gate and six installable, data-preserving milestones, with every owner-usable milestone verified by automated acceptance evidence and a bounded checklist on the owner's Acceptance Device.

## Implementation Decisions

- Training Compass is a private, native, owner-only iPhone app targeting stable iOS 26 in Swift 6 language mode with strict concurrency checking. It has no backend, app-operated cloud storage, account system, networking feature, remote configuration, analytics, or third-party crash reporting.
- The SwiftUI app target remains a thin composition and presentation layer. It owns the Today, Cycle, Progress, and TMs destinations; navigation; scene and lifecycle integration; app-switcher concealment; dependency composition; and main-actor observable feature models.
- Non-presentation code lives in one local Swift package with focused modules named `TrainingDomain`, `TrainingInsights`, `TrainingApplication`, `TrainingPersistence`, and `HealthKitAdapter`. Dependencies point inward. Domain and insight modules do not depend on SwiftUI, HealthKit, GRDB, or filesystem APIs, and framework-specific record types do not cross application-owned interfaces.
- `TrainingDomain` owns Training Cycle, Training Week, 5/3/1 Session, lift, Training Max, prescription, result, Training Event identity, confirmation, and append-only audit rules. `TrainingInsights` owns pure e1RM, workout, running, Recovery Evidence, baseline, guidance-gating, and explanation calculations. `TrainingApplication` owns use cases and interfaces for repositories, HealthKit, clocks, calendars, timezones, UUIDs, filesystems, and logging. The adapter modules implement persistence and HealthKit boundaries.
- GRDB over SQLite is the persistence technology. The app maintains two independent databases with separate monotonic migration sequences. The authoritative database contains all Locally Authoritative Data, durable write-back intent, and append-only domain audit entries and participates in ordinary encrypted Apple backup. The reconstructible database contains the HealthKit Mirror, anchors, stream and rebuild state, late enrichment, simplified routes, and disposable projection caches and is excluded from backup.
- Both database directories use complete file protection. Locked-device access is deferred; the implementation does not weaken storage protection to obtain background Health access. There are no cross-database foreign keys or cross-store atomicity claims. Stable HealthKit UUID references remain authoritative even if their mirrored objects are temporarily absent.
- Every confirmed authoritative mutation and its required audit entry commit in the same authoritative transaction. Current records are stored normally while mandatory before/after values, timestamps, actions, decisions, and corrections remain available through a separate immutable audit ledger.
- A Schedule Template contains a reusable normal-week layout. The built-in Default Schedule initializes or resets it to Monday Squat/Bench Press, Tuesday Overhead Press/Romanian Deadlift, Thursday Bench Press/Squat, and Friday Deadlift/Overhead Press, with the first listed lift Primary and the second Assistance.
- A Training Cycle contains three fixed ordered normal Training Weeks and, after every second Completed Training Cycle, one Deload Week. Completion advances cadence even with Skipped Sessions; Abandonment does not. The app permits one Active Training Cycle and one optional Draft Training Cycle.
- A Draft Training Cycle is a snapshot copied from the Schedule Template. Activation recomputes due Deload inclusion, validates required Training Maxes, snapshots Training Maxes and Loading Increments, and resolves a stale anchor or cadence mismatch through explicit preview and confirmation. Dates are timezone-free local calendar dates.
- Calendar Changes move Scheduled Sessions without changing Training Week or prescription. Program Edits alter only Scheduled Sessions or lift roles in the fixed cycle sequence, do not rewrite completed work, and update the Schedule Template only through a separate explicit save. Completed and Abandoned cycles remain terminal historical snapshots except for audited fact corrections.
- Normal Primary prescriptions are 65/75/85 percent for five/five/five-plus in Week 1; 70/80/90 percent for three/three/three-plus in Week 2; and 75/85/95 percent for five/three/one-plus in Week 3. Deload Primary prescriptions are 40/50/60 percent for five repetitions with no Plus Set. Assistance is five sets of ten at 65 percent in normal weeks and 50 percent during Deload.
- Kilograms are the only Equipment Unit. Each prescribed set calculates from the exact Training Max and rounds independently to the nearest per-lift Loading Increment, with exact ties down. Training Maxes may be any positive kilogram value; Loading Increments default to 2.5 kilograms. A Set Result records exact positive weight and non-negative whole repetitions and may differ from its immutable Set Prescription.
- Every prescribed set must have a Set Result or an Omitted Set disposition before Session completion. Zero repetitions represents a failed Set Result. Ordered Additional Sets remain actual work outside the prescription and never create or alter a prescription.
- e1RM uses the Epley formula with a one-repetition special case. Only an eligible normal-week Primary Plus Set Result with at least one repetition produces an observation. Calculations retain full precision and display one decimal place; they are not rounded to a Loading Increment.
- After an eligible Completed Training Cycle, the app creates an independent fixed Training Max Proposal for every Progression Lift that appeared in the Primary role: plus 5 kilograms for Squat and Deadlift and plus 2.5 kilograms for Bench Press and Overhead Press. Every proposal shows relevant evidence and requires accept, reject, or manual replacement. Variants and custom lifts progress manually.
- Today leads with a planned Session and same-day Health Workouts, with Recovery Evidence subordinate to the training task. Cycle displays the complete Draft or Active Training Cycle. Progress separates Strength, Running, and unified history. TMs centralizes current and pending Training Max values, proposals, and history. These information contracts are fixed; final visual styling is not.
- `HealthSyncCoordinator` is the single actor responsible for HealthKit scheduling, trigger coalescing, cancellation, retry, background opportunity handling, and independent per-stream state machines. Bounded stream tasks may fetch concurrently, but one writer commits each delta's additions, replacements, deletions, status, and next anchor atomically. The anchor never advances if the transaction fails.
- Health Workouts, sleep, resting heart rate, HRV SDNN, and applicable Workout Enrichment are independent Health Data Streams. Each retains requested state, positively known coverage, mirror availability, reconciliation activity, last successful check, and current failure separately. Combined screens degrade by section rather than through one global Health state.
- Foreground entry, observer invalidations, manual Health Data Refresh, unlock recovery, and retries all enter the same coordinator. Observer delivery is opportunistic and callbacks are invalidation signals, not payloads or freshness guarantees. The app does not poll.
- Health Data Refresh performs non-destructive incremental reconciliation for requested streams and does not retry write-backs. Health Data Rebuild is a separately confirmed repair action that discards reconstructible state, resumes from durable checkpoints, and never changes Locally Authoritative Data.
- The HealthKit adapter imports authorized workout, sleep, resting-heart-rate, HRV SDNN, associated heart-rate, distance, energy, and route data as application-owned transfer values. It retains provenance and positive coverage facts and never claims knowledge of a hidden read-permission state. Route access and lazy fetch occur only when route functionality is used.
- A Training Event is a Health Workout, a 5/3/1 Session, or an explicit one-to-one linked pair. Linking preserves both records and source authority and counts the occurrence once. Only Training Compass's own HealthKit Write-back may reconcile automatically through sync identifier and increasing sync version. External Health Workouts require explicit linking and unlinking.
- The HealthKit Write-back is a traditional-strength summary only. Set detail, prescriptions, Training Maxes, e1RM, and audit history never leave the authoritative store through this feature. Write-back uses durable independent states, permits persistent opt-in and per-session opt-out, retries transient failures, requires explicit action for permission failures or external deletion, and cannot roll back local completion.
- Derived Projections are calculated on demand from indexed source data first. Pure insight functions return the observation and its Insight Explanation together. A projection may become a cache only after repeatable evidence shows the indexed implementation exceeds the insight budget; cache keys include algorithm and source/configuration revisions, and deleting the cache changes only latency.
- The Rolling Workout Overview uses the trailing seven local dates including today and compares them with the median of the preceding four non-overlapping seven-day periods. It keeps workout frequency, duration, HealthKit activity types, and Heart-Rate Zone time separate, leaves distance and energy activity-specific, and always exposes coverage.
- Heart-Rate Zones use the owner's explicit current maximum heart rate and fixed 50–59, 60–69, 70–79, 80–89, and 90–100 percent bands, with below 50 percent separate. Only sample-associated intervals of no more than 60 seconds contribute; uncovered edges and longer gaps remain unavailable. Configuration changes recalculate historical projections without changing raw samples.
- Running Performance includes only source-classified running Health Workouts and preserves Outdoor, Indoor, Treadmill, or Unspecified environment. Average Running Pace is positive duration divided by positive distance, calculated at full precision and displayed as minutes per kilometre to the nearest second. Run Date is fixed from the start time using source timezone metadata or the device timezone captured at first import.
- Running Volume keeps count, available positive duration, and available positive distance as separate seven-day measures against the four-period median. A Comparable Run requires positive distance and duration, identical source-owned environment, and full-precision distance within an inclusive five percent of the selected Running Reference Run. Comparison uses the immediately preceding match and, only when four exist, their median.
- Comparable Run heart rate is a covered-interval time-weighted average and is compared only when each participating run has at least 80 percent duration coverage. A Running Comparison Exclusion is a reversible authoritative decision keyed to the exact HealthKit UUID; it affects comparable trends only and never history or Running Volume.
- Recovery Evidence is limited initially to Primary Sleep duration, duration consistency, timing consistency, resting heart rate, and HRV SDNN. Sleep stages and Naps remain descriptive. Preferred Sleep Source selection prevents overlapping sources from being summed. Intervals within 90 awake minutes form episodes; the longest episode ending on a wake-up date is Primary Sleep and the rest are Naps.
- A Personal Recovery Baseline is the median and middle 50 percent of valid daily observations in the preceding 28 calendar days and requires at least 14 observed days. Missing days are not zero-filled or carried forward. Current values are classified below, within, or above the band with boundaries counted within; this is personal context, not a normal range or target.
- Primary Sleep and all sleep-derived measures form one Recovery Evidence Family; resting heart rate and HRV SDNN form one family each. Recovery Guidance requires at least two baseline-established families and a current, successfully reconciled, comparable observation for every baseline-established family. A family without any established baseline does not block guidance.
- Recovery Observations and Recovery Guidance use neutral factual language. The app may enumerate aligned or conflicting values and invite the owner to consider how they feel. It does not vote, weigh signals, assign confidence, diagnose, predict performance, claim causality, label recovery, or prescribe or preselect a training change. Guidance is independently disableable.
- Every derived view ships with an Insight Explanation naming the question, included dates and records, sources, coverage, grouping or calculation rule, comparison baseline, configuration, exclusions, missing information, and last reconciliation time.
- A Training Compass Export is a versioned UTF-8 JSON document with a manifest, integrity digest, complete authoritative data and stable identities, schema and generator versions, and a readable summary. An optional separately labelled HealthKit Mirror snapshot is reference material only. The initial export is unencrypted, created only after a sensitive-data warning, shared through the system sheet, and deleted from temporary app storage afterward.
- Import is replacement recovery rather than merging. It validates and migrates into staging, checks relationships and domain invariants, and performs a recoverable same-volume swap only after the replacement is complete. Failure or interruption preserves either the original or the complete validated replacement, never a partial mixture.
- Full App Erasure removes both local stores, preferences, projections, sync state, and temporary exports after explicit confirmation. Optional deletion of Training Compass HealthKit Write-backs occurs first and offers retry or acknowledged local erasure after partial failure. The feature never deletes another source's workout or claims to erase device backups, iCloud backups, retained exports, or HealthKit copies outside its authority.
- The implementation observes the fixed Verification Data Envelope: 15 years of Health history; 25,000 workouts; 10 million heart-rate samples; 250,000 sleep intervals; 50,000 resting-heart-rate samples; 100,000 HRV samples; 500 cycles; 10,000 Sessions; 250,000 sets; and 2,000 lazily fetched routes of at most 2,000 retained points. The envelope is a verification scale, not a retention cap.
- Acceptance Device budgets include 1.5 seconds for 95th-percentile cold launch, 500 milliseconds for foreground resume, 150 milliseconds for a durable local mutation, 300 milliseconds for an ordinary local query, 750 milliseconds for a complex insight, and no continuous main-actor work over 100 milliseconds. HealthKit wait time is measured separately from app-controlled processing.
- Health import uses at most two concurrent streams, one database writer, and one pending batch per active stream. A batch is capped at 5,000 records or 4 MiB decoded data, targets transactions under 250 milliseconds, and keeps transient import buffers below 32 MiB. Full-envelope processing, memory, storage, migration, route, background, and Device Energy budgets remain hard release evidence obligations as defined by the resolved performance decision.
- Installation uses one stable explicit App ID and bundle ID under free Xcode Personal Team signing. A per-user day-five reminder runs preflight checks and leads to a one-click but attended in-place refresh. The workflow may automate build, signing refresh, profile inspection, installation, launch smoke testing, and result logging, but never stores Apple credentials or promises unattended renewal or data preservation without verified export and restore.
- Delivery follows Gate 0 plus six coherent milestones: Engineering Foundation; Local Training Core; Health Workout Foundation; Unified Events and Enrichment; Training and Running Insights; Recovery Evidence and Guidance; and Write-back and Release Hardening. Gate 0 is not approved for owner data. Milestone 1 adds export, validated import, and Full App Erasure before becoming the first owner-data build. Every later milestone preserves and migrates prior data and hides incomplete future capabilities.
- Correctness, durability, migration, privacy, memory, and foreground responsiveness failures block progression. Performance or Device Energy waivers are limited to documented OS or HealthKit variance and require measurements, affected scope, owner-visible effect, expiry, and explicit owner acceptance; a waiver never silently changes a budget.

## Testing Decisions

- The confirmed primary behavioral seam is the composed `TrainingApplication` use-case boundary. Scenario tests exercise application-owned use cases with real `TrainingDomain` and `TrainingInsights` behavior and temporary real GRDB stores while injecting deterministic HealthKit, clock, calendar, timezone, UUID, filesystem, logging, resource-pressure, and device-service fakes. This is the highest practical seam that proves orchestration, domain behavior, persistence, cross-store ordering, durability, explanations, retries, and recovery without depending on nondeterministic device services.
- Tests assert externally visible behavior and durable state: returned application snapshots, committed records, emitted explanations, audit history, stream status, retry state, file lifecycle, and owner-visible failure outcomes. They do not assert private helper calls, SwiftUI view structure, raw database implementation details beyond published invariants, or framework internals.
- Pure `TrainingDomain` and `TrainingInsights` tests remain the exhaustive seam for combinatorial rules that would be unwieldy at the composed boundary: lifecycle transitions, calendar boundaries, prescription and rounding rules, snapshot semantics, e1RM eligibility, rolling windows, comparable-run thresholds, sleep episodes, baseline boundaries, guidance gates, and deterministic explanation content.
- Persistence integration tests use real temporary SQLite databases for schema constraints, authoritative mutation-plus-audit atomicity, Health delta-plus-anchor atomicity, observations, one-writer behavior, backup attributes, staged replacement, migrations, failure injection, and termination at every recoverable phase.
- HealthKit adapter contract tests are narrower supplements. Most Health behavior uses a scripted semantic fake of the application-owned HealthKit interface rather than attempting to mock `HKHealthStore`. Focused adapter tests prove translation to and from real HealthKit types, authorization request behavior, anchored query handling, observer registration, route pagination, and write-back metadata.
- Critical XCUITests cover only the highest-value owner journeys through stable accessibility identifiers: first launch and Health connection, cycle creation and activation, Today set logging and completion, Training Max Proposal decisions, degraded Health recovery, and export/import recovery. Domain permutations remain below the UI seam, and final styling is not snapshot-gated.
- Acceptance Device checks prove behavior unavailable to simulator or deterministic fakes: actual HealthKit authorization and queries, observer and foreground reconciliation, complete file protection, backup exclusion, app-switcher concealment, Personal Team installation and in-place refresh, optimized-build performance, memory, storage, thermal behavior, route work, Device Energy, and interruption/recovery under device conditions.
- A version-controlled acceptance matrix maps every critical domain rule and transition to its source decision, preconditions, deterministic seed, expected external result, automated layer, device-evidence requirement, and latest evidence. Each applicable use case covers success, boundary, missing or partial data, interruption and retry, and privacy or recovery. Not-applicable variants require explicit justification.
- Checked-in fixtures are synthetic and deterministically seeded. Owner-derived workouts, health samples, routes, UUIDs, dates, set results, and notes never enter source control, even when described as anonymized. Generators and generated manifests carry schema and algorithm versions and cover readable examples, boundaries, failures, historical schemas and exports, and the complete Verification Data Envelope.
- The HealthKit fake models independent streams, partial authorization outcomes without claiming hidden read denial, anchored pagination, replacements, deletions, invalid anchors, delayed or unavailable enrichment, observer bursts, cancellation, background expiry, lock and unlock, route outcomes, write authorization, version replacement, external deletion, restoration, and deterministic call history.
- Privacy checks reject undeclared networking or network-capable dependencies, analytics or crash SDKs, unexpected entitlements, packages outside the allowlist, sensitive fixtures or logs, framework records crossing application boundaries, missing file protection, incorrect reconstructible backup exclusion, excessive or prohibited diagnostics, unconfirmed exports, incomplete cleanup, unsafe erasure, deletion of another source's workout, and app-switcher leakage.
- There is no global line-coverage threshold. Coverage is diagnostic; every defect fix needs a regression scenario, and acceptance-matrix completeness is the correctness gate.
- Gate 0 establishes a stable non-interactive command contract: bootstrap prerequisites; verify formatting, boundaries, domain, insight, application, persistence, adapter, and privacy behavior; run critical UI journeys; regenerate fixtures; verify all migrations; run a named milestone device smoke check; run the complete release protocol; and build an inspectable evidence index. The established command names are `make bootstrap`, `make verify`, `make test-ui`, `make fixtures`, `make verify-migrations`, `make device-smoke MILESTONE=<id>`, `make verify-release`, and `make evidence`.
- Every change runs the general verification command. Navigation or critical-journey changes also run UI tests; schema or export changes run migration verification; fixture changes regenerate deterministic artifacts. Each owner-usable milestone additionally installs in place, executes its short Acceptance Device journey and a degraded or failure recovery case, verifies storage protection and backup exclusion, records privacy-safe environment metadata, and proves local training remains usable while Health work is unavailable or active.
- Prior art is the repository's resolved implementation-gates decision, acceptance-matrix scenario families, migration/performance budgets, HealthKit capability research, and reviewed core-workflow prototype. The repository is planning-only and contains no production test suite to imitate; Gate 0 creates the first executable test conventions without weakening those already resolved behavioral contracts.

## Out of Scope

- Public App Store distribution, paid Apple Developer Program signing, Ad Hoc distribution, and TestFlight.
- Accounts, authentication, multiple users, social features, sharing between users, leaderboards, coaching portals, or app-operated synchronization.
- A backend, hosted database, CloudKit data store, remote configuration, analytics, advertising, or third-party crash reporting.
- A companion Apple Watch app. Workouts recorded by Apple Watch and available through Apple Health remain in scope as Health Workouts.
- Nutrition, calorie targets, meal logging, body-composition coaching, or general wellness tracking outside the defined workout and Recovery Evidence set.
- Manual entry of workouts other than 5/3/1 Sessions.
- Arbitrary strength-program percentages, additional progression systems, mixed Equipment Units, pounds, or plate-inventory optimization.
- Automatic Training Schedule changes, automatic Training Max changes, automatic external-workout linking, or any other training decision made without explicit owner confirmation.
- Readiness, recovery, fitness, training-load, or combined cross-activity scores; medical diagnosis or treatment advice; anomaly, injury-risk, illness, or performance predictions; causal claims; and prescriptive Recovery Guidance.
- Personal records, goals, goal achievement, race equivalents, inferred race results, route similarity, route heat maps, named locations, split-pace derivation, or inferred running environment.
- Editing source-owned HealthKit workout facts, inferring missing Health data, treating unavailable read results as proof of denied access, or deleting workouts written by another source.
- Using HealthKit Write-backs as backup for Training Compass's detailed local history.
- Cross-device merge, app-operated backup, encrypted export in the initial format, or guaranteed forensic erasure of Apple backups, shared exports, HealthKit copies, or removed application storage.
- A separate Face ID or app-specific passcode gate in the initial product.
- Guaranteed real-time HealthKit freshness, continuous polling, background completion deadlines, or notifications for ordinary reconciliation delays.
- Automatic retention limits or data deletion when the Verification Data Envelope is exceeded.
- Final visual styling, typography, color, animation, and production polish beyond the fixed information hierarchy, accessibility, privacy, and behavioral requirements.

## Further Notes

- The domain glossary is normative. Implementation, tests, UI copy, and future issues should use its exact terms, including Training Schedule, Schedule Template, Training Cycle, 5/3/1 Session, Health Workout, Training Event, Locally Authoritative Data, HealthKit Mirror, Derived Projection, Recovery Evidence, Personal Recovery Baseline, and Insight Explanation. Avoided aliases in the glossary should not re-enter implementation language.
- The fifteen resolved Wayfinder decisions remain the detailed decision record behind this synthesis. When this spec is less precise than a resolved decision, the resolved decision governs. Implementation must not reinterpret a product rule or hide a newly discovered product decision inside code; unresolved product choices return to planning.
- The reviewed prototype establishes information hierarchy and interaction priorities but deliberately does not establish final visual design.
- Research statements about HealthKit, Apple signing, and recovery interpretation were current when their decision tickets were resolved. Before implementation relies on a platform detail that could have changed, verify it against current official Apple documentation without expanding product scope.
- The later owner decision to use free Personal Team signing supersedes the earlier research recommendation to use paid development signing for sustained use. The operational expiry and attended-refresh risk is accepted and must remain visible in product and runbook language.
- The roadmap is complete only after Milestone 6 passes and every acceptance-matrix row has current evidence. An installable milestone is not complete merely because its screens exist.
